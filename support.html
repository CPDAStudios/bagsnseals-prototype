Frequently asked

How many bags are included on a roll?+

Each continuous Bags n’ Seals roll contains 40 flexible liners.

How does the sealing mechanism work?+

Gather the filled liner and guide it through the dedicated slot. The internal tape mechanism closes the bag before removal.

Where is the replacement supply stored?+

The continuous liner roll sits in a separate lower compartment built into the waste container.

Which colors are available?+

The current plastic system is offered in white, gray and black finishes.

Can I purchase bags and sealing tape separately?+

Yes. Bag rolls, multi-roll refill packs, case quantities and sealing-tape refills are available through the online store.

Where is Bags n’ Seals made?+

The current Bags n’ Seals system is made in the United States.
